# Cashflow calculator

This module adapts the Cashflow calculator from personal-finance-dashboard into a browser-local, full-page feature. The [route](../../routes/cashflow/+page.svelte) owns editable inputs, synchronization with the [settings controller](../settings/README.md), the display-period preference and the contextual rail that sets it, and composition of [the net-income, balance-sheet, and income-breakdown views](../components/views/cashflow/). Annual model values use the shared [USD formatter](../text/README.md) for display. The [income model](income.ts) owns normalization, income-mode conversion, and calculations; it has no browser state, listeners, timers, or teardown.

## Calculation contract

The initial illustration is $120,000 annual gross income, a 24% effective tax rate, $12,000 in annual retirement contributions, and no other deductions. Its annual net income is $79,200 and monthly net income is $6,600. Hourly assumptions start at 40 hours per week and 52 working weeks per year.

| Quantity | Calculation |
| --- | --- |
| Annual gross | Annual salary, or hourly rate × hours per week × weeks worked |
| Annual taxes | Annual gross × effective tax rate / 100 |
| Annual net | max(0, annual gross − annual taxes − retirement contributions − other deductions) |
| Monthly net | Annual net / 12 |

The reference's policy is retained: empty, invalid, nonfinite, and negative input values read as zero, and net income has a zero floor. The effective tax rate must be a plain percentage from 0 through 100: a value outside that range, or one written with letters such as `0.24k`, reads as zero and is flagged rather than clamped. All six numeric inputs and mode conversions use the [shared number-input adapter](../calculator/presentation/number-input.ts), backed by Scratchpad’s lexer. Dollar signs, comma/underscore digit separators, and case-insensitive k/m/b/t suffixes are accepted, including `$125k`. Percentage inputs take percentage points, such as `24`, without a `%` sign or magnitude suffix. Expressions and declarations remain in Scratchpad. Retirement contributions and other deductions are subtracted after estimated taxes; they do not reduce the tax basis. The effective tax rate is supplied by the user, with no tax-bracket or payroll-rule calculation. Overflow while annualizing valid finite inputs returns an explanation instead of displaying infinite results. Text that is present but does not parse as one number, such as `$10km` with its stacked suffixes, still reads as zero, but `invalidCashflowFields` reports it, and an unreadable tax rate, with the problem for each, so the presentation can warn rather than silently showing a zero line item. Blank and negative values, and hourly assumptions while annual income is selected, are not reported.

Switching annual/hourly modes converts the current income using the saved work assumptions. The implementation retains numerical precision rather than rounding an intermediate hourly rate to cents, so repeated mode changes do not progressively change take-home pay. A blank income stays blank; converting an annual amount without positive working hours produces zero, following the reference. An overflowing conversion preserves the existing inputs and mode. Values stay unrounded until USD display.

## Route state and presentation

The route renders deterministic defaults during SSR, adopts the root controller’s saved `cashflow_inputs_v1` working copy on mount, and writes subsequent edits to the working copy in the root-owned [settings controller](../settings/README.md), backed by the [storage primitive](../state/README.md). Normalization accepts only the expected field types and retains intentional empty strings. Editing state belongs to the mounted route, while its saved working copy belongs to the root settings controller and one browser profile; there is no account, remote API, or cross-tab synchronization. The shell's initialization spinner hides default/restored-state changes before revealing the view.

Inputs occupy the left of two even desktop columns, with the headline net income directly beneath them; the balance sheet and income breakdown occupy the right column and follow the inputs on small screens. Hourly assumptions appear when hourly mode is selected. The shared `OptionSwitch` highlights the selected Annual/Hourly label while its track keeps a neutral color, matching the Amount/Percentage control in Amortization. Clear empties all six numeric inputs while keeping the income mode; Reset restores the saved Settings preset, including mode and work assumptions; the reference illustration applies until a custom preset is saved. Both have pointer and keyboard hover text and share the same persisted state as ordinary edits. Fields supply the dark ground required by the shared form-control contract. Fields reported by `invalidCashflowFields` are marked `aria-invalid` and described by a `WarnNote` beneath the fields that names each one. The route passes the same list to the summaries: the headline net income and balance-sheet net income turn red, each line item fed by an invalid field turns red with a `WarnIcon` and screen-reader text, and the income breakdown warns that the shares it draws are counting an unreadable value as zero. Invalid income or work hours flag gross income and taxes; an invalid tax rate flags taxes.

The Annual/Monthly `OptionSwitch` lives in the route's contextual rail, initially Annual, because the period is no longer one card's concern: it retitles and divides the balance sheet, retitles and divides the headline net income, and selects the amounts the income breakdown reports on hover. Monthly divides each annual figure by twelve before currency formatting, without changing the model. Displayed cents can differ slightly when summing individually rounded monthly rows. This presentation choice persists separately under `cashflow_monthly_balance_v1`, is restored on route mount, and survives both individual and global calculator resets. The rail also carries the shared [Show tooltips control](../components/charts/ChartHoverToggle.svelte), which reads and writes the root controller's `chartTooltips` preference rather than a Cashflow-local cell, so the choice is the same one Amortization's rail shows; with it off the pie neither accents the wedge under the pointer nor displays its tooltip. The rail carries the assumptions `InfoNote` that previously closed the page too, so the page ends with its figures; notes inside cards use their available container width. The route registers this rail on mount and calls the release it is handed back on teardown.

The income breakdown draws the same four figures as a pie of shares of gross income, using the shared [`PieChart`](../components/charts/README.md). Gross income is the whole, so the four slices are exactly the figures that divide it and their shares are the same in either period; only the hover amounts follow the period. What is kept leads the pie and its legend in the page's emerald, then the deductions in ascending order over a red ramp assigned by size, so the deepest red always marks the largest deduction and a shade follows the magnitude rather than the line item. A legend divider separates what is kept from what was taken out. When taxes and deductions exceed gross income, the model's zero floor means the four figures total more than the whole they would be shares of; the breakdown then replaces the plot with that explanation rather than drawing shares of something else. It says the same when the calculation overflows or gross income is zero, and warns above the plot when an unreadable input is being counted as zero.

Extend the model when a new feature changes the financial rules, a feature component for its presentation or transient interaction, and the route for composition, rail registration, or persistence. Shared input-card framing remains in the reusable component layer, while income fields and financial summaries retain their feature contracts. Tax-basis adjustments, payroll calendars, additional contribution modes, or negative-net reporting require an explicit change to the calculation contract and reconciliation tests.

## Validation

The [model tests](../tests/cashflow/income.test.ts) cover the reference illustration, annual/hourly equivalence, fractional work assumptions, the tax basis, zero-floor behavior, normalized storage, invalid values and their reporting, and finite-number boundaries. Run the [application gates](../../../README.md#run-and-validate) and exercise mode conversion, persistence, Clear/Reset, the rail period selection and its effect on all three summaries, slice hover and its accent, both themes, mobile layout, navigation, and startup rendering in the browser.
